What Is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator is a simple online tool that calculates your exact age based on your date of birth. It shows you how many years, months, and days you've been alive. Beyond just your age in years, it can also calculate the total number of days, weeks, hours, and minutes you've lived, as well as when your next birthday will occur.

This tool is useful for various purposes, including verifying your age for online services, understanding your exact age for personal interest, or simply satisfying your curiosity about how much time has passed since your birth.

How to Calculate Age

Calculating age manually can be complex due to varying month lengths and leap years. To calculate your age accurately:

  1. Start with your birth year and subtract it from the current year to get your age in years
  2. Check if your birthday has occurred this year; if not, subtract one year
  3. Count the months from your birth month to the current month to get additional months
  4. Count the remaining days from your birth day to today to get the days component
  5. Account for leap years (divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400)

Our Age Calculator handles all these calculations automatically, accounting for leap years and different month lengths, ensuring complete accuracy.
